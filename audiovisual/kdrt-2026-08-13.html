about the show

Yolo Backyard Bulletin features local news, commentary, music, gossip, and true stories from Yolo County, working to rebuild community communication in the public sphere. Guests come from a wide variety of backgrounds to share news and views about what's happening locally, engaging respectfully and constructively even where there's disagreement.

Hosted by Jesse Drew. Airs live Thursdays 5 to 6pm on KDRT 95.7 FM ↗, Davis, with a replay Sundays 8 to 9am.
