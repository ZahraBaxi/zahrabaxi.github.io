On San Pablo


On San Pablo is a photography project that captures the stretch of San Pablo Avenue, a major street in the East Bay from Oakland to Hercules. Created for GEOG 189: Visual Geography at UC Berkeley, the project comes from weekly field days spent walking, observing, and photographing different segments of the avenue.
Students in the course must compile their own sequence to tell a story about the environment and human presence on this street.
