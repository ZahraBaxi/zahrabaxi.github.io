The Lit Review Project


HELLO, QUICK NOTE! This is an ongoing project that follows along with the progress of my Masters Thesis. This page currently does not reflect all of the zines I've created so far. Most of my current zines are still in progress or are being user tested. I hope to update this page shortly with some more of those zines, however enjoy what is currently publicly uploaded! Full project of 20+ zines will be uploaded by June 2026!

The Lit Review Project is an ongoing series of visual literature reviews that merge research, design, and reflection. What began as a way to reframe my master’s thesis reading into something more personal and tactile became a creative methodology of its own! Each zine translates a single book or theme from my literature review list into illustrated spreads, hand-drawn diagrams, and fieldwork-inspired layouts. All zines are designed to be printed and folded at home (you'll find a download link below each example, along with for most zines instructions within with gallery), making them accessible and interactive for readers. Also feel free to also browse the full list of literature for my thesis on Goodreads ↗ if you're interested in reading any of these yourself!
