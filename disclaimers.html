disclaimers

some housekeeping about this site.

privacy

I use Google Search Console occasionally on the backend for this site. While Google does collect some data through Search Console, I don't have access to any personally identifiable information. For transparency the data it provides to me are page clicks and main countries visitors come from. I do not recieve more than that. I also do not utilize Google Search Console for this purpose. I use it make sure my site is properly indexed within the web, making it easier for users like you to find them. You can learn more about their privacy policy here: Google Privacy Policy ↗

forms & data

The contact and bug report forms on this site use Back4App ↗ to store submissions. That data goes into a private database that only I can access. I use it to reply to you or fix the thing that's broken, nothing else. What you write and submit is what I will see and nobody else has access to it.

general disclaimer

This site is a personal portfolio and creative space. I share projects I've worked on and my processes and thoughts behind them. I've worked in design spaces for a long time and sometimes my thoughts about projects change over time, I try my best to keep them updated or leave a reflection on how they relate (or no longer relate) to my current practice.
