overview

Stebbins Cold Canyon is a beautiful hiking spot, near Lake Berryessa, a part of the University of California Natural Reserve System. It also has a big on going issue that has been increasing in importance each year, heat related health risks. In the summer of 2025 alone, the reserve had 6 emergency helicopter rescues due to heat exhaustion. As part of gaining experience within park spaces I've collaborated with the UC Reserve Systems within the Davis campuses on several projects for specific reserves. This project was focused on Stebbins Cold Canyon, and the goal was to create a heat awareness campaign to educate visitors about the risks of extreme heat and how to stay safe while enjoying the reserve.
I worked with Professor Emily Schlickman from the Landscape Architecture + Environmental Design department to create a campaign that would be effective in communicating the risks of heat and providing actionable steps for visitors to take to stay safe. The campaign included a variety of materials, sandwich board signs, stickers, ground stakes, and signage, all designed to raise awareness about the dangers of heat and encourage visitors to take precautions when visiting the reserve.
