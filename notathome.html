Not At Home


A Zine for EDUC 140 AC

I created a zine for my class EDUC 140 AC during Spring 2022 as a final project. In the class I was asked to reflect course readings to my own experiences. I wrote and drew about my experiences as a brown queer woman growing up in the United States. You can view it below or on my Behance ↗
